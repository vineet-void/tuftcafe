About TuftCafe

Where art meets craftsmanship

Our Story

TuftCafe was founded in January 2025 in Jaipur, Rajasthan by Tamanna, a Fine Arts graduate with a lifelong love for creating. Art has always been instinctive — from painting and crafting as a child to exploring different creative mediums over the years. Discovering tufting felt natural. The transformation of yarn into textured form was both exciting and deeply satisfying. What began as curiosity quickly turned into commitment. TuftCafe was built to create handmade pieces that carry intention, individuality, and strong artistic identity — not mass-produced décor, but work that feels personal. The long-term vision is to grow TuftCafe into a creative studio space in India where people can gather, learn tufting, and experience handmade art in a meaningful way.

Our Craft

Every TuftCafe rug is custom-designed and handcrafted by one artist — from concept development to final finishing. Most pieces are made-to-order. Some clients come with complete designs; others share an idea, a reference, or simply a feeling. The design is refined collaboratively before production begins. Precision matters. Clean carving, balanced composition, and durable finishing are essential parts of the process. Customers often appreciate the detailing — because each rug is treated as functional art, not just a product. A TuftCafe rug is created to transform a space — adding character, warmth, and personal expression.
