Care Instructions

Regular Cleaning

Vacuum your rug once or twice a week using a gentle setting. Avoid rotating brush heads as they can damage the fibers.

Initial Shedding

New tufted rugs may shed slightly during the first few weeks. This is completely normal and will gradually reduce.

Spills & Stains

Blot spills immediately using a clean cloth. Avoid rubbing the stain to prevent spreading.

Deep Cleaning

Handmade rugs should not be machine washed. For deep cleaning, professional rug cleaning is recommended.

Sunlight Exposure

Avoid placing rugs in strong direct sunlight for long periods to maintain color quality.

Storage

If storing your rug, roll it instead of folding and keep it in a dry environment.
