How To Get Your Custom Rug

From idea to masterpiece — handcrafted by TuftCafe.

STEP 1

Share Your Idea

Choose a design from our collection or send us your own sketch, inspiration image, or concept. At TuftCafe, we turn your vision into a custom handcrafted rug made just for you.

STEP 2

Design Approval

Our team connects with you to finalize size, colors, and details. A digital design preview is shared for your approval before production begins.

STEP 3

Hand Tufting Process

Once approved, we begin crafting your rug using carefully prepared yarn and skilled hand-tufting techniques to bring your design to life.

STEP 4

Finishing & Quality Check

Your rug is trimmed, detailed, and thoroughly inspected to ensure it meets our quality standards before being safely dispatched to your doorstep.
