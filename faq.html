Tufted rugs are made with wool yarn, and light shedding during the first few weeks is completely normal.

  • Vacuum gently once or twice a week (no rotating brush).
  • Do not machine wash or soak the rug.
  • Blot spills immediately — avoid rubbing.
  • Trim loose threads carefully with scissors.
  • For deep cleaning, opt for professional cleaning only.
  • Wool Yarn: Soft, durable, and gives a premium finish.
  • Tufting Cloth: High-quality base fabric for precision and strength.
  • Backing: Felt or anti-slip backing may be added for extra support and grip.

All materials are carefully sourced to ensure durability and long-lasting quality.
