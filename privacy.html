Privacy Policy

At TuftCafe, your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Placing an Order

Our website does not offer direct checkout or account registration.

If you wish to place an order, you contact us directly through the provided contact options. We collect only the information necessary to process your request.

Information We Collect

We collect personal details only when you voluntarily share them while placing an order, such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Shipping address
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Payment details required to complete your order
We may also receive basic technical information like device type or browser data to improve website performance.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Process and manage your order
  • Arrange delivery
  • Communicate updates
  • Provide customer support
We do not sell, trade, or misuse your personal information.

Information Sharing

Your details are shared only when necessary to complete your order, such as with delivery partners or payment processing services. Only essential information is shared.
