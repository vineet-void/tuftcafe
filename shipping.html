Shipping Policy

At TuftCafe, every rug is handmade after order confirmation.

Below is how our shipping process works.

Order Confirmation & Production

  • 50% advance payment is required to confirm your order.
  • Production starts once the advance is received.
  • The remaining 50% balance must be cleared before dispatch.

As each rug is made-to-order, production typically takes 10–14 business days.

Shipping Process

Once your rug is ready and full payment is completed:

  • Your order is carefully packed and dispatched.
  • We work with reliable courier partners for delivery.
  • Tracking details will be shared after dispatch.

Delivery Time

Delivery timelines vary depending on your location.

While we aim to dispatch within the committed timeframe, delivery may sometimes be affected by courier delays or unforeseen circumstances.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges (if applicable) are communicated during order confirmation.

Costs may vary based on rug size, weight, and delivery location.

Delays

Delivery times may be affected by holidays, customs clearance, or unforeseen courier delays. We are not responsible for delays caused by third-party shipping partners.
